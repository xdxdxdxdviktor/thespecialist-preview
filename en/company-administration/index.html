PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

You may be wondering.

Can we handle several changes at once?

Yes. We first assess how they relate to each other and agree on a joint procedure.

Do you also handle legal acts?

Where specific expertise is required, we coordinate with a lawyer, notary or other specialist. Their scope and price are set out in the offer.

Why might you ask me for identification?

Where required by law, we identify and verify the client before providing the service, in accordance with Czech Act No. 253/2008 Coll. We only request the data necessary to meet our legal obligations, and only after the offer has been accepted.
