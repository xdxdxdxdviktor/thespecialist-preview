ABOUT THE SPECIALIST

A partner for decisions.
And the steps that follow.

The Specialist s.r.o. guides entrepreneurs from company formation through company changes, transfers and transformations to closing the company.

HOW WE THINK

Your situation first.
Our offer second.

A good approach starts with understanding the context – what you need to solve, what you already have and what matters to you.

Our job is to help you get oriented, define the scope of cooperation and coordinate the agreed steps. Specialised questions are handled with the relevant experts according to the brief.
