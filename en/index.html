REFERENCES

Real cases.
Real results.

Selected cases from our practice.

Company liquidation

Completed

KSM – Krok Start mobil, spol. s r.o.

Situation
A Czech company had foreign directors and shareholders who decided to close it for good. The whole process had to be handled in the Czech Republic without the clients dealing with each step in person.
Solution
We took over coordination of the liquidation – legal and accounting steps, communication with authorities, statutory notices, settlement of the company and the final documentation.
Result
The liquidation was properly completed and the company was deleted from the Commercial Register.

Company liquidation

Final stage before deletion

VECTOR ENERGY, s.r.o. v likvidaci

Situation
The sole shareholder of the Czech company was the German Anfeya GmbH. The Czech liquidation had to be aligned with the foreign owner, including accounting and legal settlement.
Solution
We acted as liquidator and coordinated the entire process including accounting, financial statements, tax obligations, calls to creditors, settlement of liabilities and communication with the shareholder.
Result
All liabilities have been settled and the liquidation balance is CZK 0. The liquidation is complete and documents for deletion from the Commercial Register are being prepared.

Company liquidation

In progress

Hookahteka s.r.o. v likvidaci

Situation
The company's directors and shareholders are based in Poland and decided to close the Czech company. The liquidation had to be handled in the Czech Republic including the related legal, accounting and tax steps.
Solution
We act as liquidator and coordinate the whole process – communication with the foreign shareholders, steps towards the register and creditors, and cooperation with the accounting firm and tax adviser.
Result
The liquidation is running smoothly with excellent cooperation from all parties and is heading towards proper completion and deletion of the company.
